Schedule your social media from one calendar

A social media scheduling tool by Vixi Agency

Postia lets creators, businesses and agencies connect their social media accounts, plan posts and videos on a calendar, and publish them automatically at the right time.

Log in to Postia

What you can do with Postia

  • Connect your accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X and other platforms using each platform's official sign-in.
  • Write a post once, attach images or videos from your media library, and adjust it for each channel.
  • Schedule posts on a calendar or publish them right away, with each platform's own settings such as privacy and visibility.
  • See the status of every post and basic analytics for your connected channels.
  • Work with your team on the same calendar.

YouTube

When you connect a YouTube channel, Postia uses YouTube API Services to upload the videos you schedule to that channel, with the title, description and privacy status you choose, and to show you the channel's analytics. Postia only acts on your channel when you schedule or publish content. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle data from Google, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Your data

We only use data from your connected accounts to publish the content you schedule and to show you analytics about it. We do not sell your data, and you can disconnect your accounts and delete your data at any time. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
