Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Postia is a social media scheduling tool operated by Vixi Agency. We only use the data from the social accounts you connect to publish the content you schedule and to show you analytics about it. We do not sell your data, and you can disconnect your accounts and delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Postia (available at https://postia.me) is operated by Vixi Agency, based in Mexico ("Vixi Agency", "we", "us" or "our"). Vixi Agency is the party responsible for the processing of your personal data under this Privacy Policy.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at admin@vixi.agency.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account data

  • Name, email address and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Organization and team information, such as team members and their roles.
  • Preferences such as time zone and language.

2.2 Content you create

  • Posts, captions, comments, hashtags and scheduling settings you write in Postia.
  • Images and videos you upload to the media library or attach to a post.

2.3 Data from connected social accounts

When you connect a social media account (for example TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn, X, Reddit or Google Business Profile), you authorize Postia through that platform's official login (OAuth). Depending on the platform and the permissions you grant, we receive:

  • Access and refresh tokens that let Postia act on your behalf. These are stored on our servers and never shown to other users.
  • Basic profile information: account ID, username, display name and profile picture, so you can recognize the account inside Postia.
  • Account statistics, such as follower, like and video counts, and metrics for posts published through Postia, to show you analytics.
  • A list of your recent posts or videos and their status, so we can confirm that a scheduled post was published.

We only request the permissions needed for the features described above. We do not access your private messages, contacts or payment information on any social platform.

2.4 Technical data

  • Log data such as IP address, browser type, and the date and time of requests, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Cookies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the service: publish your posts at the scheduled time, upload your media to the platforms you selected, and show you the status and results of your posts.
  • To manage your account and team, and to authenticate you.
  • To provide optional AI features (such as writing suggestions), only when you use them.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix technical problems.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use data received from social platforms to build profiles of you or of your audience.

4. Platform-specific information

4.1 TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, Postia uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. With your authorization, we access:

Permission What we use it for
user.info.basic, user.info.profile Show your TikTok avatar, display name and username in your Postia channel list.
user.info.stats Show follower, like and video counts in Postia analytics.
video.upload Send a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, when you choose that option, so you can finish it in the TikTok app.
video.publish Publish a video to your TikTok account at the time you scheduled, with the privacy and interaction settings you chose.
video.list List your published videos to show post status and per-video analytics.

Postia never publishes to TikTok without an explicit action from you. You can revoke Postia's access at any time from the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or by disconnecting the channel in Postia.

4.2 YouTube and Google

Postia uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read channel analytics, and Google Business Profile APIs to publish posts to your business profile. By connecting a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Postia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Postia's access at any time from your Google security settings.

4.3 Facebook, Instagram and Threads (Meta)

Postia uses Meta's official APIs to publish content to the Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Threads profiles you connect, and to read insights about that content. You can remove Postia's access at any time from your Facebook or Instagram settings under "Apps and websites", or by disconnecting the channel in Postia. See section 8 for data deletion instructions.

4.4 Other platforms

For LinkedIn, X, Reddit and any other platform you connect, we use the platform's official API only to publish the content you schedule and to read statistics about it. Each platform's own privacy policy also applies to your use of that platform.

5. How we share your data

We only share your data in these cases:

  • With the social platforms you choose: when you schedule a post, its content and media are sent to the platforms you selected so they can be published.
  • With service providers who help us run Postia, under confidentiality obligations: our hosting provider (Hostinger, where our servers and database are located), our DNS provider (Cloudflare), and, only when you use AI features, our AI provider (OpenAI), which receives the text you ask it to work on.
  • With your team: members of your Postia organization can see the channels, posts and media of that organization according to their role.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or of Vixi Agency.

Some of these providers process data outside Mexico, for example in the United States or the European Union. When that happens, we take reasonable steps so that your data remains protected as described in this policy.

6. How we protect your data

  • All traffic to Postia is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access tokens from social platforms are only used by our servers.
  • Access to our servers and database is restricted to authorized personnel.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if a security incident affects it.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account data and content are kept while your account is active.
  • Tokens and data from a social account are deleted when you disconnect that account from Postia, except for posts already published through it, which stay in your post history until you delete them or your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer to comply with the law.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time for security purposes.

8. How to delete your data

  • Disconnect a social account: in Postia, open the channel's menu and choose to disconnect or delete it. This removes its access tokens from our servers.
  • Revoke access from the platform: you can also remove Postia from the app permissions of TikTok, Google, Meta or any other platform, as described in section 4.
  • Delete all your data: email admin@vixi.agency from the address on your account with the subject "Data deletion request". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

9. Your rights

Under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, you have the right to access, rectify, cancel or oppose the processing of your personal data (ARCO rights), and to withdraw your consent. Depending on where you live, you may have similar rights under other laws, such as the GDPR or California privacy laws.

To exercise any of these rights, email admin@vixi.agency with your name, the email of your Postia account and a description of your request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and in any case within 20 business days.

10. Children

Postia is intended for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with an updated date, and if the changes are significant we will notify you by email or inside Postia.
